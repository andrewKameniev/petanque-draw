# Swiss + Barrage + Playoff

Extended competitive format with a round-robin qualification stage between Swiss and Playoff.

## Flow

1. [Swiss](./swiss.md) rounds determine initial ranking
2. [Barrage](./barrage.md) — qualifying teams play round-robin in small groups
3. Group winners advance to [Playoff](./playoff.md) knockout bracket

## Optional additions

- [Cadrage](./cadrage.md) can be inserted between barrage and playoff for additional filtering
- Tournament B for non-qualifying teams

## When to use

Large tournaments (30+ teams) where the organizer wants maximum accuracy in determining playoff qualifiers. The barrage eliminates lucky Swiss results — teams must prove themselves in direct round-robin competition.
