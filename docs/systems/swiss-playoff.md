# Swiss + Playoff

The most common competitive format. Swiss rounds determine seeding, then top teams play a knockout bracket.

## Flow

1. [Swiss](./swiss.md) rounds (typically 4-7 rounds depending on team count)
2. Organizer triggers "Go Playoff" — top N teams (power of 2) enter bracket
3. [Playoff](./playoff.md) — single elimination to determine final ranking

## Qualification

- Teams qualifying for playoff are highlighted in the ranking table
- Number of playoff teams is configured in preferences (4, 8, 16, 32, 64)

## Optional: Cadrage

Add a [Cadrage](./cadrage.md) play-in round between Swiss and Playoff. See [Swiss + Barrage + Playoff](./swiss-barrage-playoff.md) for the full combination.

## Optional: Tournament B

When playoff starts, non-qualifying teams can be moved to a new independent Swiss tournament ("Tournament B") so they continue playing.
